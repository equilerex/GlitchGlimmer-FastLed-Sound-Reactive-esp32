## Context

ESP32 sound-reactive LED firmware. PlatformIO, board `ttgo-t1`, FastLED on pins 25/33, TFT_eSPI on 18/19/5/16/23, INMP441 I2S mic on 26/27/32. `src/` is also the audio analysis and the animation library, and three entry points drive it: `src/main.ino` on the board, `src/sim_main.cpp` as the host harness, `src/wasm_main.cpp` in the browser.

`ARCHITECTURE.md` holds the shape and the load-bearing decisions, and the previous Context is in `archive/2026-09.md`. Read `ARCHITECTURE.md` before touching the audio feature model, the compositor or the brightness curves, because most of what looks like a tuning constant in those places is a rule with a reason behind it.

Where this stands. Both environments build, CI runs both, and the harness is green. Nothing has run on hardware, which is the headline open item, so do not describe the firmware as working.

`web/` was rebuilt as an instrument panel in ten reviewed tasks, per `plans/2026-09-18-led-strip-visualizer.md`. **Read `web/viz/CONTEXT.md` before touching the renderer** — it carries the four-input model and the two rules most easily broken by a well-meaning change. The two that cost the most to rediscover: continuity is emergent through `fuses()` (`sigma > pitch`) and must never become a per-type branch in the lit path, which was already caught once in review; and `app.js` and `live.js` each construct their own `StripView` on one canvas, so binding is repeatable and the stale view must be detached or its pointer handlers corrupt the pose. `npm test` runs 26 checks on `node --test`, which raised the Node floor to 22 because it no longer accepts a directory argument. The approved design lives in the repo at `web/design/mockup-reference.html`; the page follows its language, not its content. The browser's live WASM renderer now separates fixed device capacity from runtime software length/count; length and pitch are software controls, while physical board macros remain unchanged.

Three things a cold session would otherwise re-derive. The only real microphone capture is `docs/glitchglimmer-2026-09-17T20-27-18-691Z.f32` and it is gitignored, 2.3 seconds long, which is shorter than the windows the measurements now use. The harness's allocation counts exclude `String`, because `sim/stubs/Arduino.h` aliases it to `std::string` and small-string optimisation hides the per-frame churn the device would pay. And the browser's pixel counts come from `Config.h` live but from `web/data/manifest.json` for recordings — they agree by convention only, which is logged in `BACKLOG.md`.

Open questions that are the owner's call rather than defects, and the visualiser's deferred follow-ups, are logged in `BACKLOG.md`.
