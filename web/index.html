What you are looking at

These frames are not a JavaScript rewrite of the animations. The host harness in src/sim_main.cpp compiles the same LEDStripController, SceneDirector and layer code the firmware ships, drives it with a scripted audio timeline over a clock the harness owns, and records the resulting pixels. The page replays those bytes, so the recording and the firmware cannot drift apart.

It is a recording rather than a live simulation: the audio timeline is fixed, and nothing here runs the FFT, the I2S driver or the display.
